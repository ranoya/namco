My  São Paulo Drizzle,

- Sad emblem of suffering -

A black man's coming ... he's white!

Coming closer, he turnes black,

He passes and turns white.

 

My drizzly São Paulo,

- London of fine fogs -

A poor man's coming ... he's rich!

Coming closer he turns poor,

He passes and turns rich.

 

My São Paulo drizzle,

- Seamstress of the damned -

A rich man comes, a white man comes,

They are always white and rich (523)...

 

Drizzle, fall from(538) my eyes.

 

(English translation by

 Jorge Luiz Antonio

 and

 Chris Daniels, USA)
