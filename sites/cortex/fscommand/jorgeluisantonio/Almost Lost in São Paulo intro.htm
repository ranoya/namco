Introduction

    (A)l(m)ost (in) S(ã)o Pa(u)l(o) is supposed to be a collaboration for 

Plural Maps: Lost in São Paulo -   http://www.lucialeao.pro.br/pluralmaps/,

 by Lucia Leão, presented in 15th Biennial of São Paulo, in 2002.

 

    As it wasn't possible to finish it on time, I forgot the project in my PC, decided to learn more HMTL and web design, and time passed.

 

    Now it is almost an individual project but it is like a tribute to Plural Maps for the invitation to collaborate in it represented a quite important motivation to go on my poetic experiments.

 

    I can say that (A)l(m)ost (in) S(ã)o Pa(u)l(o) is a kind of hypertext poem made by images and words.

                                                                    Jorge Luiz Antonio
